# Site review, November 2026

A full pass over the repository at `8719828`: code, visualisations, design, SEO
and agent surfaces, facets and search, tooling and data. Per `docs/README.md`
this file lives in `audits/` while it has open findings.

## How to read this

- **Method.** Six parallel reviews, each told to measure rather than opine and
  to stay inside the brief (`.impeccable.md`, `DESIGN.md`). Numbers come from a
  fresh production build (Node 24, `npm run build`, then `check:build`, unit and
  lifecycle suites: all green), parsed HTML of all 218 pages, and Playwright runs
  at 320 to 1440 in both themes.
- **Verified vs reported.** Items marked **✔** I re-checked myself after the
  reviewers reported (reproduced in a browser or read in source and build).
  Everything else is a reviewer's measurement that I have not repeated. Fixes
  are located by reading source; **none has been implemented or tested**.
- **Not run.** The e2e suite, Lighthouse, `check:agentic`, lint. Nothing was
  checked against the live site, Google Search Console or Scholar.
- **Priorities.** P0 = a reader hits it today. P1 = clear value, small or
  medium cost. P2 = worth doing. P3 = optional. Effort S/M/L.
- **One side effect to know about.** The site loads Google Analytics on
  `localhost` (see 7.1). Before that was noticed, roughly 600 page loads of the
  local build ran without Google's hosts blocked, from headless Chromium on
  2026-11-11. The explicit `page_view` is suppressed on localhost, but gtag's
  own events may have reached the production property. Later runs blocked the
  hosts.

## 1. Bugs

### 1.1 Back to a filtered index loses the filter (P0, S) ✔

- **Repro.** `/publications`, click `Books` (`?type=book`, 4 of 51), open a
  record, press Back. The address bar still reads `?type=book`; the list shows
  51 and the chip is unpressed. Reproduced on the build. Same on
  `/conference-activity`, `/activities` and the search box (`?q=`).
- **Cause.** `src/lib/actions/urlFilterSync.svelte.ts:119` writes with
  `replaceState`. Kit stores the pre-filter `page.url` in the history entry and
  restores that on popstate, putting the real address on `page.shallow.url`. The
  URL-to-filters effect (`:132`) reads only `page.url.searchParams`.
- **Fix.** Read `(page.shallow?.url ?? page.url).searchParams`. Untested.
- **Test gap.** `tests-e2e/filters.spec.ts:34-49` covers Back only from a
  deep-linked start, where no `replaceState` ran. Add click → record → Back.

### 1.2 Nine publications are typed `Book` (P1, S) ✔

`resolvePublicationJsonLdType` (`src/lib/utils/entityJsonLd.ts:101-121`)
switches on the source directory. `bulletins` and `proceedings` have no case and
fall to `default: return 'Book'`. Five bulletin notes and four proceedings
papers (JCDL and CIKM among them) are emitted as books with `numberOfPages`,
lose `isPartOf` and `pagination`, and carry
`<link rel="type" href="https://schema.org/Book">`. The `bulletin-article`
branch under `articles` is dead. Fix: add both cases, make the default throw,
and add a test that no non-book maps to `Book`. While there: chapters could be
`Chapter` and special issues `PublicationIssue`.

## 2. SEO and scholarly metadata

### 2.1 Titles (P1, S) ✔

`truncateTitle` (`src/lib/utils/seoUtils.ts:233-252`) returns the stem before
the first colon for any title over 50 characters. Result: 152 of 217 titles end
in `…`; 19 groups of pages share a title (`Beyond Keywords… | Frédérick Madore`
on five pages); a talk, its paper and its activity post are indistinguishable.
The same string goes to `og:title`. Fix: stop pre-truncating (Google truncates
by pixel and ranks on the full text), add a family suffix where a talk and a
paper share a title, and assert title uniqueness in `check:structured-data`.

### 2.2 Internal linking (P1, S–M)

Pagination is client state over `<button>`s, so the prerendered indexes link
12 of 51 publications, 18 of 85 talks, 8 of 37 activities. `/cv` links none of
its 136 entries to their record pages. Over the whole build, **36 records have
no inbound `<a>`** (29 talks, 4 publications, 3 DH projects) and 74 have one.

Cheapest first:

1. Link each CV entry title to its record (the id is already in the CV view).
2. Render the full list on the indexes. At 51 and 85 rows a pager costs more
   than it saves: Ctrl-F, print and "copy the bibliography" all stop at page 1.
   Publications grouped under year headings is the natural form.
3. Add an orphan gate to `check:prerender`: every sitemap URL needs one inbound
   link from another page.

### 2.3 Publication metadata (P1, S–M)

- **No external identity in JSON-LD.** `sameAs` is on 0 of 51, though
  `wikidataId` exists on 45, a DOI on 33 and `openAlexId` on 13. Add all three.
- **Language is a display string.** `inLanguage: "French"`,
  `citation_language: French`. Use BCP 47 codes through one shared map.
- **Proceedings (4)** have no `citation_conference_title` and no pages;
  **the encyclopedia entry** has no `citation_book_title`. The fields exist.
- **`citation_pdf_url` on 1 of 51, and off-host.** Scholar wants the PDF beside
  the abstract page. Thirty publications are flagged open access and the site
  hosts none of them. Self-archiving those PDFs is the largest Scholar lever
  available; it is a content task (L).
- **11 publications have no abstract** on the page.
- Chapters list the book's editors as `DC.creator` (`publications/MetaTags.svelte:48`);
  use `DC.contributor`. No record carries an ISSN, though `llms.txt` advertises it.

### 2.4 Highwire tags on non-papers (P1, S)

All 85 talk pages carry `citation_title` and `citation_author`; on 31 the
`citation_pdf_url` is the event's website
(`communications/MetaTags.svelte:108-111`). All 37 activity pages carry
`citation_journal_title = "Frédérick Madore"`, and the 18 DH pages carry
`citation_*` too. Scholar and Zotero treat any such page as a paper. Minimum:
emit `citation_pdf_url` only for a real same-host PDF. Better: keep Dublin Core
and COinS on those families and drop Highwire.

### 2.5 French content is not marked as French (P2, S)

`<html lang="en">` everywhere; 22 French publications and 36 French talks. The
index rows set `lang` (`BibliographyRow.svelte:279`); the record `h1`, the
abstract, `/cv`, the slides gallery and related-item titles do not. This is
WCAG 3.1.2, a language signal for francophone queries, and the precondition for
hyphenating long French titles (5.3). No `hreflang` and no French site: both
correct. A French abstract field on French-language publications would be worth
having where the journal supplied one.

### 2.6 Social cards (P2, M)

Ten pages pass an SVG or AVIF as `og:image`, which no platform renders. Every
page claims `summary_large_image`, but 38 of 50 publication images are portrait
covers. 33 pages fall back to the profile photo, including both ACM papers.
`og:image:width`/`height` on 0 pages. Fix: generate a 1200×630 card per record
with `sharp` (cover on paper ground, title, type, year), committed like the
deck posters. Interim: `summary` for portrait images; never SVG or AVIF.

### 3.2 Search

- **On-page search misses what the row prints**: the abstract excerpt, the type
  label, status ("forthcoming" → 0 although six rows print it), project,
  country. The agent search covers the full abstract, so an assistant finds
  more than a reader does. No phrase search, no match highlighting.
- **Counts do not narrow with the search.** With `q=togo`, `All 51` and every
  chip stay at corpus values; the tally and the empty state sit below the fold
  at 1440×900, so typing changes nothing visible.
- **No site-wide search.** Recommended: a `gen:search` projection (id, kind,
  title, year, people, tags, venue, short excerpt; estimated 60 to 90 KB),
  fetched on first focus and matched with the existing `matchesSearchTerms`. No
  dependency, and it satisfies `check:bundle` because it is fetched, not
  imported. Pagefind becomes the better choice only at several times this size
  and needs `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` in the CSP.

### 3.3 Vocabulary

283 distinct tags across the three datasets; 143 used once; only 18 shared by
all three. The worst split: publications say "Artificial Intelligence" (8),
talks and activities say "AI" (47), and they never meet. Others: NER / Named
Entity Recognition / Named-Entity Recognition; OCR; NLP; Volkswagen Foundation
/ VolkswagenStiftung; four spellings of universities; four of decoloniality;
`-ization` against the British-English rule. Twenty-two tags restate `type`
(Workshop, Conference, Talk); twenty more are institutions or series that want
a field. One person splits into two facet values under two credited spellings
(Schoenfeld / Schönfeld; Anato), although `people/` already holds the aliases.
A citing author splits the same way (Aboubakar / Aboubacar Sidiki Segda: 6 + 3,
third-ranked if merged).

Fix: a `tagVocabulary.ts` of canonical names and aliases, and a
`dataIntegrity` rule that every tag is canonical, so the split cannot regrow.

### 3.4 Smaller

- Empty states differ on each index, against the Clearing Rule; `Clear all`
  unmounts itself and drops focus to `<body>` (`EntityFacetGrid.svelte:305`).
- The "Filtered by" line prints values without facet names: a country and a tag
  both read "TOGO". Two tags print "ISLAM · TOGO" though the rule is OR.
- No export of a filtered set. Cheapest: a prerendered `/publications.bib`,
  then a client-side "Download .bib (N)".
- Entry points: co-author names and the rail's countries are plain text; link
  them to the filtered index. `show_publications` lacks `author`.

  but class-level rules still resolve midnight tokens. Fix: re-point the
  semantic tokens for `html.dark` inside that print block, as `Footer.svelte:352`

- **Controls print.** 77 on `/publications`; the cite buttons and nav on

### 5.3 Record titles (P1, M)

records run eight lines or more (worst: 13 lines, the whole first screen). The

and set `hyphens: auto` (needs 2.5), and keep `\d{4}–\d{4}` unbroken.

A citation dateline under the title (journal, volume, year, pages, DOI in mono)
would put the citable facts above the fold even where the Cite block is not.

## 7. Tooling and performance

### 7.1 Google Analytics on localhost and in CI (P1, S)

`utils/gtm.svelte.ts:92-98` injects gtag on first interaction or after five
seconds. The localhost guard suppresses only the explicit `page_view`: the
script still loads. Nothing in the e2e fixture, Lighthouse or `check:agentic`
blocks Google, so every local preview and CI run reaches the production
property.

Fix: return early on localhost and `navigator.webdriver`, and abort Google
hosts in the shared Playwright fixture.

### 7.2 Bundle and budgets

- **`/cv/timeline` imports the full publications dataset** ✔
  (`utils/timelineData.ts:17`): 320 of the route's 608 KiB, the heaviest on the
  site, for title, date and type. Build the items in a server load. Then lower
  the 850 KiB route budget, which is 40% above anything real.
- **Three Lighthouse budgets have almost no headroom**: `/cv` document size
  58.2 of 60 kB; font count 5 of 5; font size 336 of 360 kB. One more CV entry
  fails an unrelated PR. On `/cv` the DOI icon is inlined 33 times (54 KB raw):
  a `<symbol>` sprite fixes it.
- **56% of shipped HTML is inline CSS** (about 100 KB per page, two thirds
  unused on any one). Five sections of `ink-signal.css` have a single owner
  (combobox, record prose, specimen frame, chart table, cite block: about 620
  lines) and can move to their components with no usage tracing. This is the
  trace the backlog said was missing.
- **342 KiB of dead JS**: jsPDF's optional `html2canvas`, `canvg` and `core-js`
  are emitted and never fetched. Alias them to an empty module.
- **Talk summaries are fat**: `heroImage` and `papers[].abstract` ride in a
  chunk five route families load. Estimated 25 to 40 KiB.
- **Service worker** applies network-first caching to the 7 MB MP3s and the
  PDFs (`src/service-worker-routes.ts`); pass through range requests and media.
  Read from source, not observed.

### 7.3 CI and tests

- `ci.yml:116-118` installs `fonttools` and `brotli` unpinned on the runner's
  ambient Python: a release that changes subset bytes fails an unrelated PR.
- No `timeout-minutes` on any job; `npm ci` runs four times; three browsers are
  installed for two tests.
- The gate is defined twice (`scripts/tools/verify.mjs` and inline in `ci.yml`)
  and has diverged.
- **No guard for the "never add" rules.** Seven style tests exist; none fails
  on a new `box-shadow`, radius, gradient or `@keyframes`. The codebase is clean

- **No visual regression**, on a repo this design-led. `/style-guide` renders
  every idiom and is the natural target (both themes, two widths; baselines

- Axe covers 10 of 26 route families in daylight and 3 in midnight. Talk
  records, research pages, the timeline and the slides gallery are never
  scanned.
- Coverage thresholds sit ten points under actual (70/63/80/71 against
  80/73/90/82).
