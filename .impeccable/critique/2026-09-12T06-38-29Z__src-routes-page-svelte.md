## Findings

- [P1] **The mobile panel's last row sits on the viewport edge.** `src/lib/components/menu/MobileMenu.svelte:156` — `.mobile-nav { height: 100% }` pins the nav box to the scroll container's 812px while its content runs to 1177px, so the declared `padding: 0 var(--space-5) var(--space-6)` (line 159) and the container's `padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` (line 147) are both stranded above the overflow. Measured at 375×812 scrolled to the end: last `.mobile-nav-item` bottom **811.6px, gap 0.4px, expected 24px + safe area**. On an iPhone the CV row lands inside the home-indicator gesture zone. Fix: `min-height: 100%` instead of `height: 100%`.
- [P1] **`theme-color` is the midnight ground on every page, including in daylight.** `src/app.html:10` `<meta name="theme-color" content="#171310">` with no `media` (and `:16` `msapplication-TileColor` the same). Daylight is the default theme, so Android Chrome / iOS Safari paint the address bar warm-black `#171310` above a `#faf7ef` page on first load. The theme is a _stored choice_, not only an OS preference, so media-scoped metas alone are half the fix: emit both (`media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)"` → `#faf7ef`, `dark` → `#171310`) **and** have the bootstrap in `src/app.html:399-414` and `toggleTheme` in `$lib/stores/themeStore.svelte` set the active meta's `content` to `--color-background` for the resolved theme. Mirror it in `static/404.html`, which carries no `theme-color` at all.
- [P1] **`color-scheme` never narrows to the chosen theme, so the browser's own surfaces ignore it.** `src/styles/base/reset.css:51-53` and the inline copy at `src/app.html:51-53` both declare `html { color-scheme: light dark }`. Measured: computed `color-scheme` is `"light dark"` under `html.light` and under `html.dark`; `scrollbar-color: auto`. A reader whose OS is dark but who chose daylight gets dark scrollbars, dark form controls and a dark overscroll canvas against warm paper, and the reverse for the OS-light/midnight reader. Fix: `html { color-scheme: light }` / `html.dark { color-scheme: dark }` in both places (keep them in step — the inline block is the pre-hydration copy).
- [P1] **The print sheet was never updated for the inverted footer or the masthead.** `src/styles/base/typography.css:587-612` sets `body { color: black; background: white }` and nothing else. ## Fine as is

The home page's own typography and rhythm (six ruled sections, identical 48/12/3px triplets, lead-vs-body tiers correct, measure respected at both widths); the portrait plate (854×742 source, `srcset` + three-slot `sizes`, aspect-ratio reserved, top-aligned with the first paragraph); the asymmetric grid arithmetic at every breakpoint; reflow at 320 and at 200% desktop zoom; the pine census (3 marks on the first screen, all prose underlines, identical in both themes); midnight as a designed negative on the masthead, hero, dropdown, footer and offline strip; every hover state measured (none colour-only for a current state, footer correctly on `--color-footer-accent`); focus rings 2px pine with defensible offsets; the skip link (parks, stamps, lands focus on `MAIN#main`); the mobile panel's focus trap, Escape, focus restore, 44px rows and `inert` gating; the disclosure keyboard contract (Space/Escape/arrows/Home/End) and its `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` wiring; the offline strip's persistent `role="status"` and asymmetric offline/back-online treatment; `::selection`; tabular figures by construction in the mono voice; axe 0 violations across 8 runs.

## Declined

- **Toning down or removing the eyebrow** on the error pages and the mobile panel — the brief rules `.eyebrow` a signature Ink + Signal element; the craft-floor's eyebrow ban is overridden.
- **A motion cue on the header's scroll-hide, the dropdown, or the mobile panel** (the playbook's "keep motion coherent"): the register is print, the one permitted motion is the page-enter fade, and all three already resolve instantly or with a single short opacity step.
- **A shadow, blur or radius to separate the dropdown sheet from the page** — separation is the 1px border plus ground contrast, by design; measured `box-shadow: none` and `border-radius: 0` are correct, not omissions.
- **"More whitespace" in the footer and around the hero** — the measured problem is the opposite (the imprint is starved of measure inside a container capped too narrow), and peer-respecting density is a stated principle.
- **Widening the home reading column back toward the container width** — the 58rem shell and the 50ch measure are the Measured Line Rule working; only the _centring_ is questioned above.
- **A second accent or a muted-pine token** to distinguish the footer's hover from the masthead's — `--color-footer-accent` is pine-bright because the footer ground is ink; that is one accent on two grounds, not two accents.
- **A `prefers-reduced-transparency` / `forced-colors` pass** — out of scope for this slice and unrequested; the existing `prefers-contrast: high` and `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks are present and measured.
